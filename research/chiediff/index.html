Research question

How should we evaluate the binding poses produced by a 3D molecular generator? We considered several kinds of computational evidence, including docking scores, changes after redocking, and physical-validity checks. These metrics capture different properties, so we report them together. The evaluation also surfaced trade-offs in molecular geometry.

My contribution

I conceived the approach, designed and implemented the framework, ran the computational evaluations, and wrote the first draft of the manuscript.
